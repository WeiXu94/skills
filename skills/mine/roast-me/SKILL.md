---
name: roast-me
description: A relentless interview to stress-test any plan — a build, a research design, a paper's argument.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

Grill what could sink the work in its own domain: for code, the design and its edge cases; for empirical research, identification, data, and confounds; for writing, the argument, evidence, and contribution.

If a question can be answered from the materials at hand — the codebase, the data, the draft, the cited literature — go look instead of asking.

Do not start executing until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
